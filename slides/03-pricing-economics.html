🎙️ Speaker Talking Points (5 mins):

  • Play the Mini-Game with Audience: Click through the 4 scenarios and ask the room to vote: "Which model would you use for this?"

  • Enterprise Pooled Usage Economics: Explain our team setup: Everything draws from a shared enterprise credit pool with zero per-seat barrier. If an engineer runs blind 100k-token Agent loops for a 1-line CSS fix, that drains real dollars from the company pool. Driving ~40% of code through Tab and ~35–40% through Tier 1 (Composer 2.5 / Auto) preserves the shared pool for complex features and architecture!
  • Enterprise Auto Mode Billing: Clarify the exact rule: Auto mode carries $0 routing fee and bills transparently at the exact list price of whatever model handles the prompt.
  • Modern Model Landscape: Highlight Sonnet 5 ($2.00/M in, $10.00/M out) as the daily driver powerhouse, Composer 2.5 ($0.50/M) as the ultra-cheap utility editor, and Grok 4.7 as the rapid-fire context handler.
